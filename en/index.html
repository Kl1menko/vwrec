What happens after your request

We clarify the number of workers, candidate requirements, and team launch dates. After that, recruitment is launched through our database and partner agencies in the recruiting countries.

1 Vacancy analysis and recruitment launch
2 Selection and candidate verification
3 Document processing
4 Arrival and work launch
01

Vacancy analysis and recruitment launch

We form a candidate profile, agree on deadlines, and start searching through relevant sources.

02

Selection and candidate verification

Candidates undergo preliminary selection, document verification, and a basic interview. The enterprise receives profiles for final approval.

03

Document processing

We organize the preparation of all necessary documents for employment and entry, and control the process until ready.

04

Arrival and work launch

We coordinate logistics and worker arrival. After arrival, the team starts working at the enterprise.
